<!-- outcomes:start -->
!!! abstract "Learning outcomes"
    By the end of this week you should be able to:

    - interpret sensitivity and complementary sensitivity as a loop's responses to disturbance, noise and model error;
    - explain why S + T = 1 forces a trade-off, and identify the trade a given design is making;
    - relate closed-loop bandwidth to noise amplification and actuator demand;
    - evaluate a design's robustness to a stated model uncertainty.
