<!-- outcomes:start -->
!!! abstract "Learning outcomes"
    By the end of this week you should be able to:

    - translate time-domain and robustness requirements into a target shape for the loop gain;
    - design lead and lag compensators to achieve that shape;
    - re-express a PID controller as a loop-shaping design, and explain what each term contributes to the shape;
    - justify a design choice from the loop gain, the step response and the robustness it buys.
