# Week 6: Consolidation week

There is no lecture this week. Week 6 is the University's consolidation week,
and it comes at a natural break: weeks 1 to 4 have taken you round the whole
design cycle, and then through PID design and stability margins. Week 7 builds
straight on that.

So this week is for **making what you've done so far solid**. Nothing new is
introduced. The activities below help you find out what you really understand,
fix what you don't, and move your coursework on, so that you start week 7 ready
for it.

All of it is **recommended, not required**, and none of it contributes to your
grade — it's formative, like everything except the week 11 coursework. What it
earns you is feedback: the challenge marks itself as you go, and the rest shows
you what you can and can't yet explain. It's the same six hours as any other
week: four of consolidation and two of coursework.

## Recommended activities {#activities}

## Why these activities

They are chosen because they work, not because they're comfortable.

- **Retrieving beats rereading.** Trying to recall something, and finding out
  where you can't, does far more for your memory than reading your notes again.
  The challenge, "Explain it back" and "Rework, don't reread" are all forms of
  it.
- **Mixing beats blocking.** The challenge deliberately mixes the four weeks, so
  that you have to decide which idea a question needs, as you will in the
  coursework.
- **Explaining shows you the gaps.** If you can't say why something works in a
  paragraph, you don't understand it yet — and it's much better to find that out
  this week than in week 10.

## Your coursework this week

If you're on track, this is a good week to act on checkpoint 1's feedback and
start your design. If you're behind, this is the week to catch up. And if you
don't get to it this week, spread the two hours across weeks 7 to 9 — but don't
let them slide further than that.

## The laboratory

Week 6 is the last week of the Quanser laboratory window. If you haven't done
your four hours yet, this is the week to do them.
