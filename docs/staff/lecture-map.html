Consolidation week

No lecture

Purpose

Cement weeks 1 to 4 — the whole design cycle, requirements and models, PID, and stability margins — so that week 7 starts from solid ground. Nothing new is introduced. Every activity is recommended, not required.

Laboratory This is the last week of the laboratory window. If you haven't done your four hours, do them now.
