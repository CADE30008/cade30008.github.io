<!-- outcomes:start -->
!!! abstract "Learning outcomes"
    By the end of this week you should be able to:

    - determine closed-loop stability from the open-loop frequency response, using the Nyquist criterion;
    - read gain, phase and delay margins from Bode and Nyquist plots, and relate them to the step response;
    - explain what a stability margin does and does not guarantee;
    - assess how actuator rate limits and time delay erode a loop's margins.
