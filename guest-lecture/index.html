Your week¶

The usual six hours:

  • The lecture, on Tuesday: 2 hours.
  • Follow it up, 2 hours: read or watch whatever the speaker recommends, and write a design log entry on one thing from it that bears on your design.
  • Coursework, 2 hours: the brief will be out this week, so read it and start your design log.
