# The Blackboard reading list, as exported

The unit's reading list lives in the University's reading-list system and is
surfaced to students through Blackboard. This folder holds a dated export, so
that the site can point at the list and describe what is on it without the two
silently diverging.

| File | Exported |

The list itself:
<https://rl.talis.com/3/bristol/lists/dd40d09f-6b89-46de-becd-40a3d365a0e6.html>
— the UUID in that URL is the CSV's filename, which is how an export is matched
back to the list it came from.

**Export a fresh copy each year**, in the annual update, and delete nothing:
the old exports are the record of what a given cohort was pointed at.

## What is on it, as of the 2026-09-21 export

Seventeen items. The ones marked **Recommended** by the list itself:

- Cook, *Flight Dynamics Principles* — the flight-dynamics half's text
- Stevens, Lewis and Johnson, *Aircraft Control and Simulation*
- Dorf and Bishop, *Modern Control Systems* — ours
- **Control System Lectures** (Brian Douglas) and **Control Bootcamp**
  (Steve Brunton), both video series
- Control Tutorials for MATLAB and Simulink
- MATLAB Academy: Control Design Onramp

Marked **Further**: Etkin and Reid, Prouty, Hancock, Beard and McLain, Nise,
Ogata, Franklin, the MATLAB Academy course list, and *Automatic Flight Control
Systems*.

## Why the site does not reproduce it

Three reasons, and they are the same reasons the schedule is moving to
Blackboard:

- **It changes without us.** Library holdings, editions and links are
  maintained centrally; a copy on the site is a copy that goes stale.
- **Links are institutional.** `readinglists.bris.ac.uk` items do open for
  anyone — Talis serves a public "shared item" page, checked 21 Sep 2026 — but
  what they lead to is the library's holdings, which are not. The list is a
  Bristol artefact even where its URLs are not.
- **It is per-cohort.** The site is evergreen; the list is this year's.

So the site names the few things worth naming, says what each is *for*, and
sends students to the list for the rest.
