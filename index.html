CADE30008 Flight Dynamics & Advanced Control¶

These pages hold the lecture handouts, slides, example sheets and worked solutions for the control half of CADE30008 Flight Dynamics & Advanced Control, at the University of Bristol.

They assume you have taken the year 2 unit Dynamics and Control of Linear Systems, or equivalent. You should be comfortable with Laplace transforms, transfer functions, Bode plots and the standard step-response specifications.

Start here

Preparing for Control checks that your laptop, tablet or MATLAB set-up can run the course code — a few minutes, and worth doing early. It also points at the MathWorks Onramp courses and a short diagnostic quiz, both recommended rather than required.

Each lecture has:

  • Handout. The full written treatment, with derivations, worked examples and interactive applets. Read it before or after the lecture.
  • Slides. The deck used in the lecture. It opens in your browser. Press F for full screen and P for presenter view.
  • Other materials. Code, examples, etc. as available. Additional elements that use internal tools appear on Blackboard, be sure to check there too.

Code appears in both MATLAB and Python. Pick your language on any code block, and the whole site will switch to it.
